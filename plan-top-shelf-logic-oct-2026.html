01

What we found

Your new site sells SEO, GEO and AI answer visibility well. The gap is that topshelflogic.com is sending search and AI engines mixed signals about itself, and a prospect checking your own rankings before they buy will see that first. All three findings below come from a public review of the site on October 1, 2026.

1. Two sites, one brand, split authority

Rankings, links and AI citations are divided between two versions
High impact

The new site runs on www.topshelflogic.com. The old WordPress site still answers on topshelflogic.com without the www, for example /meet-the-team/, with its own canonical tag and an index, follow robots tag. Google and AI engines can treat these as two separate sites, each with its own team page, nav and service pages.

topshelflogic.com (old WordPress)Self canonical, index, follow. Nav links to 60+ legacy URLs incl. 28 mass tort pages
www.topshelflogic.com (new site)31 URLs in sitemap. Different page slugs for the same services
→301 map
One canonical siteEvery legacy URL 301s to its closest new page, mass tort pages kept or merged by traffic, one sitemap, one brand entity

2. Your best proof is invisible to crawlers

AI answer engines quote what is in the HTML, not what animates in
High impact

The counter stats on the homepage and the Fractional CMO page start at zero and animate up with JavaScript. In the HTML a crawler reads, your headline proof says $0M and 0x. Many AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, so the numbers you most want quoted are the ones they cannot see.

StatWhat a crawler readsWhat a visitor sees
Client revenue generated$0M+$750M+
Return on investment0x33x
Years of expertise0+21+
Google rating0.0 stars5.0 stars (31 reviews)

Fix: put the final values in the HTML and animate from there, then add Organization and AggregateRating schema so answer engines get the numbers in a structured form.

1

Consolidate topshelflogic.com onto one canonical site

Crawl both versions, map every legacy URL to its closest new page and 301 it. Decide which mass tort pages to rebuild on the new site, based on their traffic in Search Console.

Why: puts the old site's links and rankings behind the pages you sell from today.

2

Make the proof crawlable and add entity schema

Render the final stat values in the HTML. Add Organization, Person, AggregateRating and FAQ schema, with one consistent sameAs list.

Why: this is what ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews use to describe and recommend you.

3

Write the SEO, AEO and GEO playbook for the junior team

One checklist for technical, on page, local, content and AI answer optimization, with QA steps. Train the juniors on it in short daily reviews.

Why: every client gets the same standard, whichever strategist runs the account.

4

Audit your top client accounts and rank the fixes

Starting with law firm, home services and healthcare clients: tracking, indexation, map pack and money keywords, sorted by revenue impact.

Why: the juniors work on what moves client revenue first, not what is easiest.

5

Set a weekly scorecard per client

Rankings, map pack, AI citations, leads and calls, fed into your command center dashboards.

Why: you see progress every Monday without chasing anyone for it.
